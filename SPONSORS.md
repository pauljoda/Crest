## How recognition works

- Sponsor and Sustainer listings may include one approved public link.
- Supporter listings use the member's Ko-fi or Patreon display name.
- Members at every level can have a sponsor flair on
  [r/CrestBrowser](https://www.reddit.com/r/CrestBrowser). Ask through Ko-fi
  or Patreon with your Reddit username.
- Members can remain anonymous or ask through Ko-fi or Patreon to change or
  remove a listing.
- Listings are normally updated within 30 days and remain while the membership
  is active.
- A listing is a thank-you. It is not an endorsement, partnership, ownership
  interest, support contract, or way to influence the roadmap.

View the membership tiers on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pauljoda/tiers) or
