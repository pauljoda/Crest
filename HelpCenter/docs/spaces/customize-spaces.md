# Customize and arrange Spaces

Open a Space’s context menu or **Settings → Spaces** to edit it. A strong visual identity makes a profile boundary obvious before you type a password or open a work account.

## Choose an identity

Crest offers two presentation styles:

- **Banner** uses Crest Studio: begin with a template, then choose a shape, field division, pattern, trim, and heraldic artwork. Use independent colors for the parts of your design, or make an identity with an emoji, monogram, or searchable SF Symbol.
- **Traditional Gradient** blends one to three colors with direction, intensity, and optional texture.

The result appears in the Space picker and browser chrome on Mac, iPad, and iPhone.

The editor keeps a preview visible as you work. Adjust the background and colors alongside the crest so the complete Space feels coherent.

## Customize folders

Choose folder color intensity and text color in the Space’s appearance editor. Individual folder menus let you set colors, symbols, and emoji. You can show a custom icon on its own instead of the animated folder artwork, and optionally match folder titles to their artwork color.

## Customize the browser around your Spaces

Open **Settings → Look and Feel** for device-wide appearance: a left- or right-side sidebar, borderless pages, tab shape and scale, pinned-tab layout, address-field colors, and default page zoom from **25% to 500%**. Presets and a live preview help you find a starting point; each changed setting has its own reset.

Choose an app icon palette here too. These controls change the browser on this device; a Space’s identity belongs to that Space.

## Reorder Spaces

Drag a Space in the picker or reorder it in Settings. Position matters for **Control-1** through **Control-9**, so put frequently used Spaces first.

## Automatic cleanup

Each Space can archive unpinned current tabs after **12 hours, 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days**, or keep them until you close them. Cleanup moves tabs into the recoverable Archive; it does not silently erase them.

Pinned sites, saved tabs, folders, and tabs marked **Keep Loaded** have different roles from ordinary current tabs. Use a saved tab for something you intend to revisit, and Keep Loaded only when the live in-page state matters.
